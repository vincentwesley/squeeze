Blocks

DEFLATE cuts the stream into blocks; each picks stored, fixed or its own (dynamic) Huffman code, whichever is smallest.

    Huffman's algorithm repeatedly joins the two least frequent symbols (or subtrees) into one. Frequent symbols end up near the top with short codes; rare ones sink and get long codes. Each 0/1 on the way down is one bit of the code.

    Every level is a trade: searching harder for matches costs time and saves bytes. Levels 1–9 use zlib's own settings; 10–12 find a near-optimal parse with a cost model, which is much slower and a few percent smaller. “Browser” is your browser's built-in (native) deflate, for reference.

    Download Squeeze's PNG

    PNG predicts each row from the pixels above and to the left (the filter), then DEFLATE-compresses the prediction errors. Open a PNG to have Squeeze decode it, or any image to have Squeeze encode it.
